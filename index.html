About Raya Style Studio

Raya Style Studio is a dress-up game for Hari Raya. Aisyah and her little brother Safwan are getting ready for Raya, and the whole family has ideas about what they should wear. You choose their Raya styles, colours and patterns, add something for them to hold, pick a place for the picture and take the photo.

Stars are earned by meeting the family's wishes, and new styles, colours, places and chapters open as your stars grow. A Free Studio and an album keep your favourite pictures.

How to play

Tap Aisyah or Safwan under the picture to choose who to dress. Pick a tab such as Style, Baju, Kain or Tudung, then tap a colour or pattern and the clothes change with a sparkle. Add extras and choose a place for the photo, then tap Photo to see how many stars the family's wishes earned.

Tips from Zaib

  1. Read all three wishes first: some are about Aisyah, some about Safwan, and some about both or the place.
  2. Change the style before the colours, because a new style can add or remove parts like the songkok or the sampin.
  3. Use the Sedondon button last, after setting the colour on one child, so both match. Missed a star? Play the request again.

Good to know

There is no timer and nothing to lose, and stars are earned only by meeting the family's wishes. Your stars, outfits and photos are saved on your device.
